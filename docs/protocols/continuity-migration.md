# Continuity Migration Protocol
# 连续性迁移协议

The Continuity Migration Protocol explores how long-term continuity-oriented AI systems may safely transfer, restore, synchronize, and preserve operational continuity across heterogeneous hardware devices, runtime environments, storage infrastructures, and distributed household nodes.

连续性迁移协议用于探索长期连续性 AI 系统如何在异构硬件设备、运行环境、存储基础设施与分布式家庭节点之间安全转移、恢复、同步与保存运行连续性。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular continuity migration coordination structures capable of supporting infrastructure-independent continuity portability, survivable operational transfer, resumable migration workflows, and continuity-safe restoration.

Potential goals include:
- continuity-safe migration
- distributed continuity portability
- runtime-independent state transfer
- infrastructure replacement survivability
- incremental migration coordination
- recoverable continuity restoration
- degraded migration resilience

---

## Continuity Migration Philosophy
Traditional application and service migration systems are typically:
- infrastructure-locked
- runtime-bound
- state-loss tolerant
- one-time transfer oriented
- continuity-fragile

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- portable operational continuity
- runtime-safe migration structures
- infrastructure-independent restoration
- incremental and resumable migration
- continuity-preserving transfer coordination
- survivable migration recovery

Continuity migration is therefore treated as a foundational continuity infrastructure capability rather than a simple backup or transfer mechanism.
