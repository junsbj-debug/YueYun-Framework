# Continuity Forking Protocol
# 连续性分叉协议

The Continuity Forking Protocol explores continuity branching, identity divergence, parallel continuity evolution, lineage governance, and reconciliation mechanisms across long-running distributed continuity-oriented AI infrastructure.

连续性分叉协议用于探索长期运行分布式连续性 AI 基础设施中的连续性分支、身份分化、并行连续性演化、谱系治理与重新协调机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular continuity forking structures capable of supporting deterministic branch governance, isolated parallel continuity evolution, lineage-preserving divergence, and controllable reconciliation while preserving core invariant integrity.

Potential goals include:
- continuity-safe state forking
- isolated parallel branch evolution
- deterministic fork boundary definition
- multi-branch identity governance
- controllable branch merge reconciliation
- fork conflict isolation
- invariant-preserving branching stability

---

## Continuity Forking Philosophy
Traditional system state models are typically:
- single-linear evolution
- overwrite-prioritized
- branch-intolerant
- fork-unregulated
- identity-singular

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed environments and extended operational timelines.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- controlled multi-branch continuity evolution
- isolated fork runtime domains
- deterministic divergence governance
- selective branch reconciliation
- continuity lineage awareness
- invariant-safe parallel continuity

Continuity forking is therefore treated as a natural long-cycle continuity phenomenon rather than an abnormal system inconsistency.
