# Continuity Lineage Protocol
# 连续性谱系协议

The Continuity Lineage Protocol explores ancestry tracking, continuity inheritance chains, lineage-aware governance, evolutionary path recording, and long-term continuity genealogy management across distributed continuity-oriented AI infrastructure.

连续性谱系协议用于探索分布式连续性 AI 基础设施中的祖系追踪、连续性继承链、谱系感知治理、演化路径记录与长期连续性家谱管理机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

## Continuity Lineage Philosophy
Traditional history and identity systems are typically:
- event-fragmented
- ancestry-untracked
- runtime-decoupled
- lineage-agnostic
- short-cycle oriented

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed environments and extended operational timelines.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- first-class continuity lineage objects
- verifiable inheritance chains
- persistent ancestry preservation
- lineage-driven governance
- distributed genealogy synchronization
- continuity-safe lineage evolution

Continuity lineage is therefore treated as a foundational continuity provenance layer rather than a temporary logging or debugging mechanism.
