# Continuity Evolution Protocol
# 连续性演化协议

The Continuity Evolution Protocol explores incremental system iteration, bounded continuity progression, adaptive transformation coordination, invariant-safe evolutionary governance, and long-cycle infrastructure renewal across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性演化协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的增量系统迭代、有界连续性递进、自适应演化协调、不变量安全演进治理与长周期基础设施更新机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Continuity Evolution Philosophy
Traditional system evolution mechanisms are typically:
- disruptive and restart-oriented
- version-locked
- full-state replacement based
- short-cycle updated
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, heterogeneous, and persistently active environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- non-disruptive incremental evolution
- invariant-bounded adaptive progression
- drift-aware evolutionary iteration
- lineage-consistent evolution inheritance
- multi-branch adaptive coordination
- continuity-prioritized infrastructure renewal

Continuity evolution is therefore treated as a bounded lifelong adaptive progression mechanism rather than a discrete software upgrade event.
