# Continuity Governance Protocol
# 连续性治理协议

The Continuity Governance Protocol explores long-cycle system regulation, distributed continuity governance, evolutionary policy coordination, invariant-aligned operational constraints, and legitimacy-preserving civilization stabilization across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性治理协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的长周期系统规制、分布式连续性治理、演化型策略协调、不变量对齐运行约束与合法性保持型文明稳定化机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Continuity Governance Philosophy
Traditional governance systems are typically:
- statically configured
- centrally administered
- rule-fixed
- short-cycle regulated
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, heterogeneous, and intermittently connected environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- self-adaptive long-cycle governance logic
- distributed policy coordination
- evolutionary rule upgrading
- invariant-locked core constraints
- lineage-aware governance inheritance
- continuity-preserving policy evolution

Continuity governance is therefore treated as a persistent civilization coordination layer rather than a static administrative rule system.
