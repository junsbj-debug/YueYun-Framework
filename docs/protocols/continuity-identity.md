# Continuity Identity Protocol
# 连续性身份协议

The Continuity Identity Protocol explores persistent entity identity anchoring, evolutionary identity continuity, lineage-bound identity inheritance, distributed identity recognition, and invariant-aligned long-cycle identity stabilization across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性身份协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的持久实体身份锚定、演化式身份连续、谱系绑定型身份继承、分布式身份识别与不变量对齐长期身份稳定化机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

## Core Objective
Design modular continuity identity structures capable of supporting lifelong identity persistence, adaptive identity evolution, lineage-consistent inheritance, and distributed cross-environment identity legitimacy while preserving invariant-core identity integrity across migration, branching, and long-cycle evolution.

Potential goals include:
- persistent lifelong identity anchoring
- evolutionary identity continuity
- lineage-bound identity inheritance
- distributed cross-node identity recognition
- fork-aware identity differentiation
- invariant-aligned identity locking
- long-cycle identity stabilization

---

## Continuity Identity Philosophy
Traditional identity systems are typically:
- credential-bound
- session-dependent
- hardware-linked
- statically registered
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, branching, migrating, and heterogeneous environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- invariant-core persistent identity logic
- evolvable peripheral identity attributes
- lineage-coupled identity inheritance
- fork-aware identity branching
- migration-safe identity continuity
- distributed legitimacy-based identity recognition

Continuity identity is therefore treated as a lifelong evolving continuity essence rather than a temporary credential or static account label.
