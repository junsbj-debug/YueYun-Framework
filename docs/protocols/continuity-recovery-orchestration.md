# Continuity Recovery Orchestration Protocol
# 连续性恢复编排协议

The Continuity Recovery Orchestration Protocol explores coordinated recovery sequencing, layered restoration workflows, distributed recovery governance, and continuity-safe operational stabilization across household-centered distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性恢复编排协议用于探索家庭中心化分布式 AI 基础设施中的协调恢复排序、分层恢复流程、分布式恢复治理与连续性安全运行稳定化机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Continuity Recovery Orchestration Philosophy
Traditional recovery systems are typically:
- component-isolated
- manually coordinated
- infrastructure-dependent
- full-restart oriented
- continuity-unaware

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented distributed AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- continuity-first recovery logic
- dependency-aware restoration workflows
- distributed recovery governance
- adaptive failure-aware orchestration
- non-disruptive state reconstruction
- survivable operational stabilization

Continuity recovery orchestration is therefore treated as a system-level continuity restoration coordination mechanism rather than a simple rollback or restart procedure.
