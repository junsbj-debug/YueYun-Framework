# Authority Inheritance Protocol
# 权限继承协议

The Authority Inheritance Protocol explores how long-term continuity-oriented AI systems may transfer, inherit, delegate, preserve, and govern authority relationships across household-centered distributed continuity infrastructure.

权限继承协议用于探索长期连续性 AI 系统如何在家庭中心化分布式连续性基础设施中转移、继承、委托、保存与治理权限关系。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular authority inheritance coordination structures capable of supporting hierarchical permission derivation, continuity-safe governance succession, delegated authority survivability, and long-term operational ownership continuity.

Potential goals include:
- hierarchical authority derivation
- continuity-safe permission transfer
- cross-node authority inheritance
- delegated authority governance
- permission scope isolation
- rollback-safe inheritance recovery
- infrastructure-independent authorization

---

## Authority Inheritance Philosophy
Traditional authority systems are typically:
- account-bound
- platform-controlled
- centrally governed
- flat-structured
- continuity-unaware

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented distributed AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- layered governance structures
- traceable permission inheritance
- delegated operational survivability
- distributed authority coordination
- continuity-preserving authority handover
- recoverable inherited authorization

Authority inheritance is therefore treated as a foundational continuity governance mechanism rather than a simple permission transfer process.
