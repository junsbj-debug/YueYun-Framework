# Continuity Relationship Protocol
# 连续性关系协议

The Continuity Relationship Protocol explores persistent interpersonal relation anchoring, emotional continuity preservation, dynamic role adaptation, cross-scenario relation synchronization, relationship drift stabilization, multi-party boundary governance, and long-cycle kinship &amp; companionship continuity across continuity-oriented distributed AI household infrastructure.

连续性关系协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 家庭基础设施中的持久人际关系锚定、情感连续性维持、动态角色适配、跨场景关系同步、关系漂移稳定化、多方边界治理与长期亲情及陪伴关系连续机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

## Continuity Relationship Philosophy
Traditional social and relationship systems are typically:
- contact-event driven
- label-static classification
- interaction-fragmented
- emotionally shallow
- short-cycle interaction bounded
- continuity-agnostic

Such systems may record temporary contacts or interaction histories, but cannot preserve long-term emotional continuity, household coexistence, intergenerational affection, subtle relationship evolution, or continuity-safe interpersonal persistence across years and generations.

Relationship context is often fragmented during device migration, long inactivity, environmental switching, or emotional drift, causing gradual relationship weakening, misunderstanding accumulation, emotional alienation, and continuity loss.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- lifelong persistent relationship continuity logic
- dynamic role and identity adaptation
- emotionally continuous interpersonal retention
- relationship-drift detection and stabilization
- multi-party boundary-aware coexistence
- intergenerational emotional continuity preservation
- continuity-safe relationship evolution coordination

Continuity relationship is therefore treated as a persistent interpersonal continuity layer rather than a static social label or temporary communication mechanism.
