# Continuity Drift Protocol
# 连续性漂移协议

The Continuity Drift Protocol explores long-term state deviation, behavioral divergence, structural inconsistency, incremental offset accumulation, and continuity-safe drift stabilization across household-centered distributed continuity-oriented AI infrastructure.

连续性漂移协议用于探索家庭中心化分布式连续性 AI 基础设施中的长期状态偏移、行为分化、结构不一致、增量偏差累积与连续性安全漂移稳定化问题。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

## Continuity Drift Philosophy
Traditional systems are typically:
- short-cycle oriented
- reset-on-restart
- drift-untracked
- transient-deviation tolerant
- continuity-unaware

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed environments and extended operational timelines.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- long-cycle drift awareness
- layered differential state tracking
- bounded continuity evolution
- incremental drift elimination
- distributed drift alignment
- invariant-protected stabilization

Continuity drift is therefore treated as an inevitable long-term continuity phenomenon that requires active governance rather than passive tolerance.
