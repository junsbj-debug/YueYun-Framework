# Distributed State Convergence Protocol
# 分布式状态收敛协议

The Distributed State Convergence Protocol explores how distributed continuity-oriented AI systems may coordinate, reconcile, stabilize, and converge operational states across household-centered distributed infrastructure environments.

分布式状态收敛协议用于探索连续性导向 AI 系统如何在家庭中心化分布式基础设施中协调、调和、稳定并收敛运行状态。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular convergence coordination structures capable of supporting distributed continuity consistency, asynchronous reconciliation, degraded convergence survivability, and infrastructure-independent operational stabilization.

Potential goals include:
- asynchronous state alignment
- distributed consensus coordination
- continuity-safe reconciliation
- degraded network convergence
- cross-node operational consistency
- offline deferred state unification
- long-cycle convergence stabilization

---

## Distributed State Convergence Philosophy
Traditional distributed systems are typically:
- strongly synchronous
- centrally coordinated
- continuously connected
- short-cycle consistent
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for continuity-oriented household distributed AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- asynchronous-first convergence coordination
- decentralized consistency logic
- deferred convergence after reconnection
- degraded operational survivability
- continuity-prioritized reconciliation
- resilient convergence across node transitions

Distributed state convergence is therefore treated as a survivable continuity coordination problem rather than a simple synchronization mechanism.
