## Continuity Presence Philosophy
Traditional online/offline interaction systems are typically:
- binary online/offline labeled
- request-response driven
- foreground-active only
- session-bounded
- interruption-oriented
- continuity-agnostic

Such systems cannot represent real-world human and household continuity presence: being present but silent, online but latent, inactive but emotionally available, offline but retaining continuity, or migrating seamlessly between environments and devices.

Traditional interaction logic often resets context, discards ambient continuity, interrupts coexistence stability, and breaks long-cycle existential persistence whenever sessions terminate or connections disappear.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- non-binary layered presence modeling
- always-background continuity retention
- silent low-disturbance coexistence
- latent standby continuity preservation
- migration-aware presence handoff
- emotionally stable companionship continuity
- event-triggered contextual awakening

Continuity presence is therefore treated as a persistent existential coexistence layer rather than a temporary online interaction state.
