# Offline Resilience Protocol
# 离线韧性协议

The Offline Resilience Protocol explores how continuity-oriented AI systems may preserve operational survivability, disconnected state stability, degraded functionality, and local autonomous continuity under unstable, partitioned, or offline infrastructure conditions.

离线韧性协议用于探索连续性导向 AI 系统如何在不稳定、分区化或离线基础设施条件下维持运行存续、断连状态稳定、降级功能与本地自治连续性。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular offline resilience structures capable of supporting network-independent operational continuity, degraded-mode survivability, disconnected state stabilization, and continuity-safe reintegration after reconnection.

Potential goals include:
- offline-first operational survivability
- degraded continuity preservation
- disconnected autonomous node operation
- offline state persistence stability
- reconnection-safe state resynchronization
- network-failure fault isolation
- long-term offline continuity robustness

---

## Offline Resilience Philosophy
Traditional distributed systems are typically:
- continuously connected
- cloud-dependent
- centrally synchronized
- real-time coordinated
- continuity-unaware

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented household AI infrastructure.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- offline-first operational logic
- degraded-mode graceful survival
- disconnected autonomous governance
- local state self-stabilization
- delayed convergence recovery
- continuity-preserving degraded operation

Offline resilience is therefore treated as a foundational continuity survivability layer rather than a temporary fallback mechanism.
