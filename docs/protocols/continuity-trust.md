# Continuity Trust Protocol
# 连续性可信协议

The Continuity Trust Protocol explores persistent trust relationships, continuity authenticity verification, dynamic credibility evaluation, lineage-bound trust governance, and distributed trust survivability across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性可信协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的持久信任关系、连续性真实性验证、动态可信度评估、谱系绑定型可信治理与分布式信任存续机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular continuity trust structures capable of supporting long-term trust relationship stability, authenticity-preserving credibility validation, lineage-aware trust propagation, and distributed legitimacy stabilization across heterogeneous continuity systems.

Potential goals include:
- persistent distributed trust maintenance
- dynamic credibility evaluation
- continuity authenticity verification
- lineage-bound trust inheritance
- trust degradation detection and recovery
- distributed trust negotiation
- long-cycle trust stabilization

---

## Continuity Trust Philosophy
Traditional trust and authentication systems are typically:
- session-scoped
- credential-static
- transaction-oriented
- identity-isolated
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, and intermittently connected environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- lifecycle-wide persistent trust logic
- dynamic context-aware credibility assessment
- lineage-linked trust propagation
- graded trust degradation and recovery
- decentralized trust negotiation
- invariant-protected trust boundaries

Continuity trust is therefore treated as a long-term legitimacy coordination layer rather than a temporary access authentication mechanism.
