# Personality Continuity Protocol

The Personality Continuity Protocol explores how long-term AI identity continuity may remain persistent across devices, runtimes, and infrastructure transitions.

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented.

It does NOT define a finalized implementation.

---

# Core Objective

Create a protocol-oriented continuity structure for long-term AI personality persistence.

The protocol attempts to explore:

- persistent identity structures
- modular memory continuity
- cross-device synchronization
- local-first survivability
- infrastructure portability
- runtime independence
- continuity boundary definition

---

# Why This Matters

Most current AI systems are:

- session-dependent
- cloud-dependent
- vendor-controlled
- short-memory oriented
- infrastructure fragile

This creates major continuity limitations.

If future AI systems become:

- household-deployed
- long-term persistent
- memory-oriented
- locally survivable
- infrastructure modular

then continuity protocols may become necessary.
