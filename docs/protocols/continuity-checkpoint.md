# Continuity Checkpoint Protocol
# 连续性检查点协议

The Continuity Checkpoint Protocol explores how long-term continuity-oriented AI systems may create, preserve, synchronize, version, restore, and recover continuity-safe operational checkpoints across distributed household-centered infrastructure environments.

连续性检查点协议用于探索长期连续性 AI 系统如何在分布式家庭基础设施中创建、保存、同步、版本化、恢复与回滚连续性安全运行检查点。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular checkpoint coordination structures capable of supporting continuity-safe rollback, distributed checkpoint synchronization, incremental state preservation, and infrastructure-independent continuity restoration.

Potential goals include:
- continuity-safe checkpoint creation
- incremental continuity checkpointing
- deterministic version anchoring
- distributed checkpoint consistency
- rollback-safe continuity preservation
- degraded recovery survivability
- infrastructure-independent checkpoint portability

---

## Continuity Checkpoint Philosophy
Traditional checkpoint and backup systems are typically:
- snapshot-isolated
- infrastructure-bound
- resource-heavy
- continuity-unaware
- recovery-fragile

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- incremental lightweight checkpointing
- continuity-aware state versioning
- offline-persistent checkpoint retention
- distributed checkpoint coordination
- rollback-safe continuity recovery
- survivable restoration structures

Continuity checkpoints are therefore treated as operational continuity anchoring structures rather than simple backup snapshots.
