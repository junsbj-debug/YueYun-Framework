# Shamecloth Protocol
# 遮羞布协议

Shamecloth refers to an always-on continuity-safe privacy layer that automatically preserves human privacy, intimacy boundaries, and family-safe visibility across shared digital environments.

“遮羞布”指一种默认常驻的连续性安全隐私层，用于在共享数字环境中自动保护人类隐私、亲密边界与家庭安全可见性。

The Shamecloth Protocol explores default-safe sensitive content presentation, identity-aware privacy masking, consent-based content unlocking, multi-party dignity protection, and continuity-preserving sensitive data governance across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

遮羞布协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的默认安全敏感内容呈现、身份感知隐私遮罩、授权驱动内容解锁、多方隐私保护与连续性安全敏感数据治理机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular shamecloth structures capable of enforcing always-clothed default privacy, context-aware sensitive-content masking, consent-locked access control, and long-cycle human privacy preservation across heterogeneous, shared, and publicly exposed environments.

Potential goals include:
- default-safe shamecloth presentation
- identity-aware content masking enforcement
- consent-based sensitive content unlocking
- multi-party privacy boundary protection
- context-aware public/private view separation
- continuity-safe sensitive data governance
- long-cycle privacy retention

---

## Shamecloth Philosophy
Traditional privacy and sensitive-content systems are typically:
- opt-in only
- user-dependent and easily bypassed
- static and context-agnostic
- post-exposure reactive
- human-error prone

Such models assume perfect user caution, which fails catastrophically during accidental sharing, distracted operation, unintended projection, malicious leakage, or impulsive behavior.

They provide little protection for the moments when users forget, get careless, panic, or simply do not realize they are exposing sensitive content.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- always-on default-safe shamecloth protection
- context-aware automatic masking
- identity-aware visibility governance
- multi-factor consent-based unlocking
- continuity-preserving privacy lifecycle management
- human-error-resistant privacy enforcement

Shamecloth protection is therefore treated as a continuity-safe visibility containment layer rather than a reactive censorship or post-leak moderation mechanism.
