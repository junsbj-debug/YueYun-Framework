# Continuity Snapshot Protocol
# 连续性快照协议

The Continuity Snapshot Protocol explores how long-term continuity-oriented AI systems may capture, package, version, synchronize, restore, and preserve continuity-safe operational snapshots across distributed household-centered infrastructure environments.

连续性快照协议用于探索长期连续性 AI 系统如何在分布式家庭基础设施中捕获、封装、版本化、同步、恢复与保存连续性安全运行快照。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular snapshot coordination structures capable of supporting continuity-safe state packaging, distributed snapshot synchronization, portable restoration, and long-term continuity image preservation.

Potential goals include:
- continuity-safe snapshot creation
- versioned snapshot lifecycle management
- distributed snapshot coordination
- offline snapshot survivability
- cross-platform snapshot compatibility
- snapshot-based continuity restoration
- corrupted snapshot isolation

---

## Continuity Snapshot Philosophy
Traditional snapshot systems are typically:
- storage-layer oriented
- infrastructure-bound
- short-term retained
- continuity-unaware
- recovery-specific

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- continuity-aware snapshot packaging
- long-term versioned snapshot retention
- distributed snapshot synchronization
- offline-accessible continuity images
- cross-environment snapshot restoration
- continuity-linked snapshot structures

Continuity snapshots are therefore treated as portable continuity image structures rather than ordinary backup archives.
