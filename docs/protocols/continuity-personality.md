## Continuity Personality Philosophy
Traditional user profiling and behavioral systems are typically:
- behavior-statistic driven
- short-term biased
- situation-fragmented
- static-label classified
- overfitting-prone
- continuity-agnostic

Such systems generate temporary profiles based on recent behaviors, often unable to distinguish between temporary emotional fluctuation and stable personality essence.

They frequently over-label users, rigidly freeze personality understanding, or lose continuity consistency after migration, long inactivity, branch divergence, or environmental switching.

Human personality is not static, nor is it randomly variable.
It possesses slow evolutionary inertia, core invariant temperament traits, contextual behavioral flexibility, emotional continuity, relationship-linked expression, and long-cycle individuality persistence.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- core-invariant personality baseline locking
- slow-cycle personality evolution coordination
- continuity-safe individuality preservation
- context-aware behavioral differentiation
- drift-resistant temperament stabilization
- migration-safe personality inheritance
- branch-aware personality continuity governance
- long-cycle selfhood continuity preservation

Continuity personality is therefore treated as a slowly evolving selfhood continuity layer rather than a static user label or short-term behavioral profile.
