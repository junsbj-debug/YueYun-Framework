# Identity & Authority Coordination Protocol
# 身份与授权协调协议

The Identity & Authority Coordination Protocol explores how long-term AI identity continuity, authority boundaries, local ownership coordination, and distributed trust structures may operate across household-centered continuity-oriented infrastructure environments.

身份与授权协调协议用于探索长期 AI 身份连续性、权限边界、本地所有权协调与分布式信任结构在家庭中心化连续性基础设施中的运行方式。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular coordination structures for identity persistence, authority decomposition, local operational control, and continuity-safe trust coordination.

Potential goals include:
- persistent node identity
- continuity-safe authority structures
- local-first operational control
- decentralized trust coordination
- cross-device identity consistency
- modular permission decomposition
- infrastructure-independent identity continuity

---

## Identity & Authority Philosophy
Traditional AI identity systems are typically:
- account-scoped
- cloud-authorized
- platform-dependent
- vendor-controlled
- session-oriented

Such assumptions may become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems.

Future household-centered infrastructure may require:
- persistent runtime identity
- local-first authority coordination
- distributed trust structures
- continuity-safe operational permissions
- replaceable infrastructure trust
- portable identity continuity

Identity and authority are therefore treated as infrastructure coordination problems rather than simple authentication systems.
