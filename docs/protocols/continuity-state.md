# Continuity State Protocol
# 连续性状态协议

The Continuity State Protocol explores how long-term continuity-oriented AI systems may define, preserve, synchronize, classify, migrate, and recover persistent operational continuity states across distributed household-centered infrastructure environments.

连续性状态协议用于探索长期连续性 AI 系统如何在分布式家庭基础设施中定义、保存、同步、分类、迁移与恢复持续运行状态。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Core Objective
Design modular continuity state coordination structures capable of supporting persistent operational continuity, survivable state migration, layered state governance, and infrastructure-independent continuity preservation.

Potential goals include:
- persistent continuity states
- layered continuity state decomposition
- continuity-safe state synchronization
- distributed operational consistency
- restart-safe continuity preservation
- infrastructure-independent state coordination
- continuity state recovery mechanisms

## Continuity State Philosophy
Traditional AI systems are typically:
- session-bounded
- transient-state oriented
- runtime-scoped
- infrastructure-dependent
- continuity-fragile

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- persistent operational continuity states
- layered continuity state structures
- restart-safe state restoration
- distributed continuity coordination
- offline-capable state preservation
- infrastructure-independent continuity mapping
- survivable degraded continuity operation

Continuity state is therefore treated as a foundational invariant infrastructure layer rather than a temporary runtime condition.
