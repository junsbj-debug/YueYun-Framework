# Continuity Baseline Protocol
# 连续性基线协议

The Continuity Baseline Protocol explores invariant anchoring, reference state definition, baseline-guided stabilization, identity continuity preservation, and controlled baseline evolution across long-running distributed continuity-oriented AI infrastructure.

连续性基线协议用于探索长期运行分布式连续性 AI 基础设施中的不变量锚定、参考状态定义、基线引导稳定化、身份连续性保存与受控基线演化机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Continuity Baseline Philosophy
Traditional benchmark and reference systems are typically:
- statically initialized
- short-cycle referenced
- runtime-decoupled
- deviation-unaware
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed environments and extended operational timelines.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- living baseline state management
- invariant-locked reference constraints
- versioned baseline iteration
- baseline-driven deviation judgment
- distributed baseline synchronization
- continuity-safe baseline evolution

Continuity baseline is therefore treated as the foundational continuity reference anchor rather than a temporary initialization snapshot.
