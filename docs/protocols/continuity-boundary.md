# Continuity Boundary Protocol
# 连续性边界协议

The Continuity Boundary Protocol explores deterministic continuity scope definition, layered boundary isolation, lineage-aware containment coordination, cross-domain demarcation, and invariant-aligned boundary stabilization across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性边界协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的确定性连续性范围定义、分层边界隔离、谱系感知 containment 协调、跨域界定与不变量对齐型边界稳定化机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

## Continuity Boundary Philosophy
Traditional boundary systems are typically:
- infrastructure-bound
- statically partitioned
- hardware-segment oriented
- service-isolated
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, branching, migrating, and heterogeneous environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- continuity-first logical boundary definition
- layered dynamic containment partitioning
- lineage-coupled scope isolation
- fork-aware boundary differentiation
- cross-domain boundary reconciliation
- invariant-locked constitutional stability

Continuity boundaries are therefore treated as persistent constitutional containment layers rather than static physical segmentation mechanisms.
