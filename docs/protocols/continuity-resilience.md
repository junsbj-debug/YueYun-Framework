# Continuity Resilience Protocol
# 连续性韧性协议

The Continuity Resilience Protocol explores persistent continuity survivability, anomaly-tolerant stabilization, failure domain isolation, lineage-preserving recovery, and long-cycle operational robustness across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性韧性协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的持续连续性存续、异常容错稳定化、故障域隔离、谱系保持型恢复与长周期运行鲁棒性机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Continuity Resilience Philosophy
Traditional resilience systems are typically:
- failure-reactive
- interruption-tolerant
- short-cycle recovery oriented
- redundancy-dependent
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, heterogeneous, and intermittently degraded environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- proactively resilient continuity retention
- failure-isolated survivability protection
- graceful degradation stabilization
- lineage-preserving recovery coordination
- baseline-aligned restoration logic
- drift-compensating long-cycle robustness

Continuity resilience is therefore treated as a persistent survivability stabilization layer rather than a temporary post-failure recovery mechanism.
