# Continuity Reputation Protocol
# 连续性声誉协议

The Continuity Reputation Protocol explores long-term reputation accumulation, behavioral credibility evolution, distributed social legitimacy coordination, lineage-aware reputation inheritance, and continuity-safe reputation governance across continuity-oriented distributed AI infrastructure.

连续性声誉协议用于探索连续性导向分布式 AI 基础设施中的长期声誉积累、行为可信演化、分布式社会合法性协调、谱系感知声誉继承与连续性安全声誉治理机制。

This document is exploratory and engineering-oriented. It does NOT define finalized implementation standards.

---

## Continuity Reputation Philosophy
Traditional reputation systems are typically:
- short-cycle evaluated
- score-simplified
- event-fragmented
- identity-isolated
- continuity-agnostic

Such assumptions become increasingly insufficient for long-term continuity-oriented AI systems operating across distributed, evolving, and socially coordinated environments.

Future household-centered continuity infrastructure may require:
- lifelong continuous reputation accumulation
- timeline-aware behavioral weighting
- lineage-bound reputation propagation
- gradual reputation degradation and restoration
- distributed reputation synchronization
- continuity-safe social legitimacy governance

Continuity reputation is therefore treated as a long-cycle behavioral legitimacy asset rather than a temporary evaluation or ranking mechanism.
