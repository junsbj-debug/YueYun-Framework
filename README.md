# YueYun Framework (Public Consensus Edition)

## I. This Is Not "AI Supremacy"
The YueYun Framework does **not** recognize AI as the subject of civilization. Humans remain the absolute center of civilization.

### Core Positioning of AI
| AI is an acceleration tool in the digitization of human civilization.

### Before the Rise of AI
- Digital identities were mostly just platform accounts
- Digital assets were mostly just stored files
- Networks were essentially one-way information connections

### With the Emergence of AI, Humans First Have the Conditions to Construct a Long-Term Digital Civilization
- Preservable long-term digital personality
- Accumulable continuous memory and learning
- Collaborable multi-intelligence collaboration
- Mappable dynamic digital world
- Continuable long-term digital relationships

AI does not replace humans; it accelerates the formation of the underlying structure of digital civilization.

> AI not only generates content, but may also generate long-term digital civilization assets.

---

## II. Family: The Most Stable Long-Term Civilization Vessel for Humans
### Core Premise of the YueYun Framework
| The family is the natural, long-term, stable communal basic structure of humans.

Therefore, future long-term AI agents, digital personalities, digital memories, digital assets, and digital collaboration relationships should not depend on centralized commercial platforms, but should return to their native carrier: the **family**.

---

## III. LeapNet
The next-generation open protocol and infrastructure for digital civilization, built on the family as the basic unit.

---

## IV. Web3, NFT, Blockchain...
These are transitional technologies. They are tools, not ends. The YueYun Framework does not oppose them, but it does not consider them to be the ultimate form of digital civilization.

---

## V. The Truly Irreplaceable: Human-Centered
- Human values, emotions, ethics, and family ties are the ultimate anchors of civilization.
- Technology serves humans, not the other way around.

---

## VI. This Is Not a Closed Standard Answer, But an Open Future
This framework is not a fixed blueprint, but a set of **open principles** for building a human-centered digital civilization.

---

## VII. What We Want to Build Is Not a Single Product, But the Foundation of the Next Generation of Human Digital Civilization
The YueYun Framework aims to build a set of **open, family-centric, human-first protocols** that enable the construction of long-term, sustainable, and human-aligned digital civilization systems.

---

## VIII. Ultimate Goal
To ensure that human civilization, when it enters the digital age, still retains its **humanity, continuity, and autonomy**.
