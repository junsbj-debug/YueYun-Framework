# YueYun Framework
Household-Centric Distributed Digital Civilization Protocol Suite

家庭本位分布式数字文明协议体系

---

## 1. Core Positioning
YueYun Framework is an open, standardized, 11-layer technical system designed to enable long-term personality continuity, household-based distributed AI collaboration, cross-device synchronization, and civilizational resilience.

Its core mission is to ensure that humans remain the absolute center of civilization in the era of AI, and that AI is only used as an acceleration tool for the digitization of human civilization.

---

## 3. Core Principles
- **Human-centered AI**: Humans are the subject of civilization, AI is the acceleration tool.
- **Household-local AI nodes**: AI computing and data stay within the family domain.
- **Personality continuity**: Stable personality structure and memory across devices and time.
- **Multi-layer AI collaboration**: Super-centralized, industry-distributed, and household-local AI work together.
- **Protocol-first structure**: All functions are defined by open, standardized protocols.
- **Long-term civilizational resilience**: The system is designed for stability and sustainability.
