# YueYun Framework Code of Conduct

## Purpose

YueYun Framework is an open long-term engineering exploration project.

The goal of this repository is to encourage:

- respectful technical discussion
- collaborative engineering research
- protocol exploration
- infrastructure experimentation
- long-term continuity thinking

This project exists for open engineering collaboration, not ideological conflict.

---

# Expected Behavior

Contributors and participants are encouraged to:

- communicate respectfully
- focus on technical reasoning
- support constructive discussion
- document assumptions clearly
- welcome modular experimentation
- encourage long-term thinking
- remain open to alternative implementations

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are discouraged:

- harassment
- personal attacks
- ideological extremism
- discrimination
- deliberate misinformation
- malicious disruption
- deceptive attribution
- hostile communication
- cult-like promotion
- AI supremacy advocacy
- forced ideological enforcement

---

# Engineering-Oriented Discussion

The framework prioritizes:

- engineering decomposition
- reproducible experimentation
- infrastructure survivability
- protocol clarity
- transparent architectural reasoning

Discussions should remain grounded in constructive exploration whenever possible.

---

# Open Research Environment

This repository welcomes participation from:

- engineers
- researchers
- protocol designers
- infrastructure thinkers
- distributed systems contributors
- local AI experimentation communities

No centralized ideology is enforced.

---

# Technology Neutrality

The framework does not enforce:

- mandatory AI models
- mandatory programming languages
- mandatory infrastructure providers
- mandatory political or philosophical positions

Technology selection remains open.

---

# Long-Term Perspective

The project explores long-term human-centered AI continuity infrastructure across potentially multi-decade timelines.

Patience, modular progress, and respectful collaboration are encouraged.

---

# Enforcement

This repository currently operates as an early-stage open conceptual engineering project.

Moderation decisions may evolve alongside repository growth and community participation.

---

# Final Statement

The YueYun Framework community should remain focused on open engineering exploration, respectful collaboration, and long-term continuity-oriented infrastructure research.
